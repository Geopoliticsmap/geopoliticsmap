How is this calculated?

Each of the 58 countries is scored by its tension level (active conflict = 100, latent tension = 50, resource-key = 20, low tension = 5), averaged across all countries, and rescaled to 0–100. Every country counts equally — no country is weighted more than another. We started tracking this daily on 17 Sep 2026, so the chart grows a little more honest every day.
